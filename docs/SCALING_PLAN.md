# Scaling Plan

## Current Level: 4x ($100/leg, $600 exposure cap)

### Graduation criteria (to 8x)
- ≥ 20 live trades completed
- Live Sharpe per trade ≥ 0.5 (bootstrap CI low ≥ 0)
- Live max drawdown < 15% of capital
- No catastrophic single-trade losses (worst trade < −5% of capital)

### Tripwire (drop to 2x)
- Live max drawdown > 25% of capital
- 3 consecutive losing trades totaling > 10% of capital
- Live Sharpe per trade < 0 over 20+ trades

### Time-based review
- Review at 60 days. If not yet graduated or tripped, hold at 4x for another 60 days.
